GPS Camera – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 · App version 2.7.9

This Privacy Policy explains how the GPS Camera Android app (package com.gsabbir.gps_camera, the “App”) handles information. The App is developed by Ghulam Shabbir, Principal, GHSS Waryam Wala, Punjab, Pakistan (the “Developer”). By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. What the App does

GPS Camera takes photos and videos and adds a stamp with your location, address, map, date, time and an optional note. It can also create photo reports and Before/After reports.

3. Third-party services used while you use the App

  • Map images: the App downloads small map tiles from Esri (ArcGIS) or OpenStreetMap. These providers receive the tile position (which reveals your approximate location) and your IP address.
  • Address lookup: your coordinates are sent to the Android system geocoder (provided by Google on most phones) and, if that fails, to OpenStreetMap Nominatim, to convert coordinates into a place name and address.
  • Internet check: at start the App checks that you are online by contacting google.com and 1.1.1.1.
  • Google Maps links: when you tap “Open in Google Maps” the link opens in Google Maps or your browser.
  • Sharing: when you tap Share/WhatsApp the files are passed to the app you choose through Android’s share menu.

These services have their own privacy policies and the Developer does not control them.

4. Optional usage statistics (only with your permission)

On first start the App may ask whether you want to share anonymous usage information. If you tap Allow, the App sends to the Developer’s Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) database: a random install ID, app version, phone model, Android version, an approximate location rounded to about 1 km with the place name, first-seen and last-seen time, and the number of times the App was opened. This helps the Developer know how many people use the App and in which areas. No name, phone number, email, photo or video is sent. You can switch this off at any time in Settings → Share anonymous usage info. To ask for deletion of your record, contact the Developer (section 9) and give the Install ID shown in Settings.

4b. Device license and update check

To make sure only authorised devices use the App and to tell you when an update is required, the App reads a settings document from the Developer’s Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and checks a record for your device. For this it uses your Android device identifier (Android ID), phone model, Android version and app version, and stores a record with these values and the last-seen time on Firebase. If your device is not yet authorised, this record is created so the Developer can approve it. This is separate from optional usage statistics and is required to use the App when licensing is switched on.

4c. Support messages

If you use Settings → Contact developer, the text you write, the optional name/WhatsApp you enter, your device identifier, phone model and app version are stored in the Developer’s Firebase database so the Developer can read and reply. Do not include sensitive personal information in messages.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows banner ads from Google AdMob on the Settings, Reports and Storage screens (not on the camera screen). AdMob may collect and use your device’s advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information and ad interactions to serve and measure ads, and may use cookies or similar technologies. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) the App shows Google’s consent form first and only requests ads according to your choice. You can reset or disable your advertising ID in your phone’s Settings → Google → Ads. See Google’s policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

6. Permissions

  • Camera, Microphone, Precise location – requested when needed; you can revoke them in Android Settings.
  • Internet – for maps, address lookup, ads and optional statistics.

7. Retention and deletion

Photos, videos and reports stay on your device until you delete them (delete from the App’s Storage screen and from your Gallery). Uninstalling the App removes its private data. Optional usage statistics are kept until you ask for deletion.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and the Developer does not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Security, changes and contact

Network requests use HTTPS. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version. Questions or deletion requests: Ghulam Shabbir – YOUR_EMAIL@example.com.
